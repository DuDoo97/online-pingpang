Stroke guide

No menus. A stroke is which button you hold, how hard you swipe at the ball, and where you are. The label above the table shows what you are loaded for.

Hold left · topspin family

Blockkeep the racket still
Drivemedium swipe
Loophard swipe: heavy topspin
Smashhard swipe at a high ball
Flickswipe at a short ball: the racket steps in for you
Banana flickswipe across a short ball: sidespin
Lobfrom far back (wheel down): swipe

Hold right · backspin family

Short pushgentle touch: keeps it short
Pushmedium swipe
Fast long pushhard swipe: deep and quick
Chopfrom far back: swipe

Topspin / backspinhold left / right while you swipe

Turn the phone

Table tennis is a wide game and the racket needs room to move. Landscape gives you the full table and a longer swing.

One finger plays everything

  • Drag to move the racket. It follows above your finger, so your thumb never covers the ball.
  • Swipe up for topspin, swipe down for backspin, sideways for a flat hit. Hold still to block.
  • How hard you swipe is the power. That is the whole control scheme.
  • Tap to toss the serve. Two fingers up or down to step in or back.

Pick your level

The ball physics is the same at every level. What changes is how much the game helps you land it, how big your racket is, and how the opponent plays. Change it any time with keys 1–4.

Your hand sets the racket face at every level. Power, timing and placement are the skill.Press H in the game for the stroke guide.
